## Category A: Professional Photography, Portraits & Cinematography

### 001. `/photorealistic` — Extreme Quality Photorealism

- **Role** : Produce an image indistinguishable from a real photograph taken with a high-end professional camera.
- **Visual Features** : 8K resolution, micro-skin details (pores, fine peach fuzz), soft shadows, natural dynamic range, zero plastic look or AI artifacts.
- **AI Obligation** : Inject realism calibration terms: `hyperrealistic photographic detail, 8k resolution, authentic skin texture, natural depth of field, color graded`.

### 002. `/cinematicportrait` — Professional Cinematic Portrait

- **Role** : Create a portrait rich in emotion and visual depth, reminiscent of a scene from a Hollywood blockbuster.
- **Visual Features** : 85mm f/1.4 lens, soft blurred background (creamy bokeh), sharp focus on the eyes, subtle teal & orange color grading.
- **AI Obligation** : Enforce a wide aperture: `shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, sharp eye focus, cinematic lighting, 85mm portrait lens`.

### 003. `/studiolighting` — Three-Point Studio Lighting

- **Role** : Controlled lighting as in a professional photography studio.
- **Visual Features** : Strong Key Light on the subject, soft Fill Light to lift shadows, Rim Light to separate the subject from the background.
