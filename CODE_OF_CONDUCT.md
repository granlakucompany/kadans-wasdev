# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

## Our pledge
We make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity or experience.

## Standards
Be respectful and constructive. Unacceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, publishing others'
private information.

## Enforcement
Report issues to **wasdevcontact@granlaku.com**. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and PRs
that violate this code, and may ban offenders.
