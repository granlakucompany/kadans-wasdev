# Skill: video / image scenes

Use your own media. `video {src, in?, out?, fit? cover|contain, audio? 0-1, title?, caption?, zoom?}`,
`image {src, fit?, zoom?, title?, caption?, focus?{x,y}, backdrop? blur|none|#rrggbb, camera?{from,to,ease}, cards?[]}`.
`backdrop:"blur"` fills letterbox instead of black bars. `camera` does a real zoom/pan.
