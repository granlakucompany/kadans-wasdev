# Skill: backgrounds and overlays

Any scene or the whole video can sit on a photo/video/design asset via `background:{src,fit,opacity,dim,
zoom,tint}`. Add up to 8 `overlays:[{src,layer,blend,anim,opacity,...}]` (smoke/light/splash/shapes),
recoloured to the brand and animated (drift/pulse/spin/float/reveal/sweep).
Design assets ship in `assets/design/` (SHAPES, SPLASH — CC0).
